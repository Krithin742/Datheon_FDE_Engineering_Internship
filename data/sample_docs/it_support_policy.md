
# IT Support Policy

## Critical Support Tickets

The IT department must respond to critical support tickets within 30 minutes.

Critical tickets include complete service outages, major security incidents, and failures affecting multiple employees.

## Standard Support Tickets

The IT department should respond to standard support tickets within 8 business hours.

## Data Access Policy

Only authorized IT administrators can access restricted infrastructure documents.

Every access request must be validated against the employee's assigned clearance level.
